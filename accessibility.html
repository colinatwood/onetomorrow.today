Accessibility

Accessibility statement

A movement that says talent should rise from anywhere cannot build a site that only some people can use.

Last updated 21 September 2026.

What we are aiming for

We are working to meet WCAG 2.2 level AA. We have not yet had an independent audit against it, so treat this as a statement of effort and current state, not a certificate.

What the site does today

  • Every page starts with a skip link straight to the main content.
  • Headings run in order, without skipped levels, so screen reader navigation works.
  • Everything is reachable and operable by keyboard, with a visible focus outline on each control.
  • A larger-text control raises the base size across the site.
  • A high-contrast mode replaces the imagery with solid colour and strengthens every border.
  • A reduce-motion control removes animation and transitions, and the site also honours the reduced-motion setting in your operating system automatically.
  • Your choices are remembered in your browser between visits.
  • Images that carry meaning have text alternatives; decorative images are hidden from screen readers.
  • The layout reflows to narrow screens and to zoom without a horizontal scrollbar.

Where it falls short

Naming these is more useful than claiming they do not exist.

Translation is partial
The language selector translates the navigation and the accessibility controls. The body of every page is still English. We would rather say so than pretend otherwise, and full translation is work we still owe you.
No independent audit yet
Our conformance claim rests on our own testing. An external review has not happened.
Long charter pages
The plan and learn pages are dense and deeply nested. They are navigable, but they demand stamina. We are working on summaries.

Tell us what is broken

If any part of this site keeps you out, email join@onetomorrow.today. Describe the page and what happened, and tell us what you were using if you can. We treat access faults as defects, not requests, and we will tell you what we are doing about it.

If our answer does not satisfy you, say so plainly and we will look again.

How this site is built

The markup, styling, and behaviour are public at github.com/colinatwood/onetomorrow.today. If you want to check a claim on this page, you can read the source that backs it.
