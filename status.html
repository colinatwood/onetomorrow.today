OneTomorrow is currently a public framework under development—not a funded institution or an active government or space program. This page separates published proposals from work that has people, funding, approval, and evidence behind it.

How to read this page

Every workstream moves through the same five states.

  1. ProposedA public idea, not yet resourced.
  2. ResearchingQuestions and evidence are being assembled.
  3. Pilot-readyOwner, safeguards, budget, and measures are defined.
  4. ActiveAuthorized work is underway.
  5. CompleteResults and lessons are published.
