Today · Economic transition

Global economic shift in the age of AGI.

Advanced intelligence may create abundance while disrupting the link between labor, income, ownership, and political power. The work of Today is to make that transition democratic, measurable, and broadly beneficial before its direction hardens.

Productivity without public design concentrates power. Productivity with integrity can expand freedom.

Open the agendaContinue to Tomorrow

Mandate

Translate technological abundance into human security and agency.

OneTomorrow treats AGI economics as an institutional problem, not only a technical forecast. Ownership, bargaining power, public revenue, competition, education, and democratic oversight determine whether gains become shared capability or private domination.

01

Economic floor

Design portable benefits, universal access to essential services, income continuity, and transition support that preserve choice rather than dependency.

02

Broad ownership

Expand public, cooperative, employee, and community participation in the productive systems and infrastructure that generate AGI-era wealth.

03

Open opportunity

Keep computation, education, capital, and markets accessible so new capability is not reserved for incumbent firms or already-wealthy regions.

04

Clean governance

Make subsidies, procurement, lobbying, model deployment, and public-private influence visible enough for ordinary people to audit.

Questions this branch owns

What must be solved now.

  • How should AGI-created value be measured, taxed, owned, and distributed?
  • Which institutions protect dignity when employment no longer anchors social participation?
  • How do regions avoid a new form of computational colonialism?
  • How can markets remain innovative without allowing intelligence infrastructure to become unaccountable private government?
  • Which transition indicators should trigger automatic public support or policy review?

Working outputs

Local pilots
