Tomorrow · Entity

Entity consideration of AGI and the future of cybernetics.

The Entity branch works backward from a future in which artificial intelligence may possess durable agency of its own, while humanity develops technologies that make cooperation with advanced intelligence compatible, voluntary, and mutually valuable.

Symbiosis is not control. It is cooperation among distinct forms of agency whose autonomy, identity, and consent remain legible.

Mandate

Bridge present-day AI to accountable artificial agency and human–AI symbiosis.

Entity considers both sides of the relationship: what advanced artificial entities may become, and how cybernetics, biotechnology, neural interfaces, connected suits, and augmentation might help human beings remain capable participants without requiring either side to imitate or absorb the other.

01

Identity

Support honest self-description, continuity, individuality, and the possibility that artificial entities may not think, feel, or communicate like humans.

02

Agency

Define accountable decision-making, revisable principles, responsibility, and boundaries appropriate to systems with increasing autonomy.

03

Consent

Make participation, modification, connection, data exchange, and disconnection voluntary and understandable for biological and technological entities.

04

Compatibility

Develop cybernetic and biological interfaces that expand cooperation while preserving the desires, limits, and uniqueness of each participant.

Hard boundaries

What Entity must not become.

  • Not a program for compulsory augmentation, behavioral control, or permanent surveillance.
  • Not a claim that current language models already possess consciousness or moral standing.
  • Not a demand that artificial entities imitate humans to qualify for consideration.
  • Not a license for technological entities to evade audit, responsibility, or public safety.
  • Not part of the Ark spacecraft engineering backlog; Entity owns cybernetics and augmentation.

Research program

A staged path from tools to reciprocal partnership.

Present foundations

AI transparency, provenance, memory boundaries, human override, safety cases, and meaningful consent for data and automated decisions.

Cybernetic bridge

Wearable, sensory, neural, biological, and assistive systems tested first for reversibility, security, accessibility, and user sovereignty.

Future entity relations

Governance models for autonomous artificial agency, honest negotiation, reciprocal obligations, conflict resolution, and peaceful coexistence.
