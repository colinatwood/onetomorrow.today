Tomorrow · Entity

Entity consideration of AGI and the future of cybernetics.

Entity asks how people should relate to an AI that may one day make lasting choices for itself. It also explores cybernetics—technology that connects human bodies and machines—so people can work with advanced AI by choice and for mutual benefit.

Symbiosis means close cooperation that benefits both sides. It must never mean control, forced connection, or loss of identity.

Mandate

Prepare for AI that can make its own choices, while keeping both sides accountable.

Entity considers both sides of the relationship: what advanced AI may become, and how body-connected technology, biotechnology, brain-computer links, connected suits, and other enhancements might help people remain capable partners. Neither side should have to imitate, control, or absorb the other.

01

Identity

Allow honest self-description and individual identity. Advanced AI may not think, feel, or communicate like people.

02

Choice and responsibility

Decide when an AI can make meaningful choices, who is responsible for those choices, and what limits still apply.

03

Consent

Make participation, physical changes, connections, data sharing, and disconnection voluntary and easy to understand for everyone involved.

04

Working together

Develop safe links between people and technology while protecting each participant's wishes, limits, and individuality.

Hard boundaries

What Entity must not become.

  • No forced body enhancement, behavior control, or permanent surveillance.
  • No assumption that today's language-based AI is already conscious or entitled to legal rights.
  • No demand that future AI imitate people before it receives fair consideration.
  • No excuse for any AI to avoid inspection, responsibility, or public-safety rules.
  • Spacecraft engineering stays with Ark. Entity owns cybernetics and other technology that changes or connects with the human body.

Research program

A step-by-step path from tools to two-way partnership.

What we need now

Clear explanations of AI decisions, records showing where information came from, limits on memory, human override, safety evidence, and meaningful consent.

Safe human connections

Test wearable, sensory, brain-connected, biological, and assistive systems for safety, security, accessibility, and the user's ability to stop.

Future relationships

Create public rules for independent AI, honest negotiation, duties on both sides, peaceful conflict resolution, and coexistence.
