# Today pilot location and sponsor selection

Prepared 21 October 2026. Status: draft procedure for T01, ready for candidate intake. No location, sponsor, funding, or trial has been approved. This procedure supports the [economic transition backlog](TODAY_ECONOMIC_TRANSITION.md); it does not authorize delivery or replace local review.

## Smallest useful first step

Invite a willing local public body or community organization to describe one unmet essential need in a defined service area. Start with a bounded service and population that can be evaluated. Explain how excluded residents can still obtain emergency help and how later coverage could expand. Do not begin enrollment or promise a benefit during selection.

The project coordinator maintains the candidate register. A resident review group and an independent reviewer assess the same published criteria. These roles are vacancies until named and accepted; a proposer cannot approve their own application. Publish conflicts and recusal decisions before scoring. If independent review is unavailable, defer selection.

## Candidate intake record

Use one record per candidate and mark every unknown explicitly. Public records contain organizational details and safe aggregates only. Ask permission before naming a potential partner; keep personal contact details in the private join route. Do not collect resident identities, health records, immigration status, or household applications.

| Field | Required evidence |
| --- | --- |
| Candidate and record date | Service area, proposing organization, record owner, next review date, permission to publish organizational details. |
| Need and scope | One essential service, proposed population and duration, unmet need, source dates, uncertainty, and coverage limits. |
| Accountable sponsor | Organization, named consenting responsible officer, proposed duties, written interest, and whether interest is exploratory or formally authorized. |
| Authority | Who may authorize the service, spend funds, contract, handle data, and hear appeals; unresolved questions for qualified local review. |
| Existing provision | Current services and benefits, responsible agencies, access barriers, and possible overlap or displacement. |
| Delivery capacity | Potential operators, capacity constraints, geography, offline access, and alternatives if a supplier withdraws. |
| Finance | Cost range and basis, possible funding sources labeled committed or proposed, restrictions, and who would fund continuity. No funding claim without evidence. |
| Community voice | How residents, workers, excluded groups, and affected Indigenous communities where relevant can participate; accessible ways to dissent. |
| Evidence and evaluation | Available aggregate baseline, missing data, feasible comparison, and an evaluator independent of delivery. |
| Conflicts and risks | Financial or political interests, dependence on a single supplier, displacement, environmental pressures, and unresolved concerns. |

## Gates before ranking

Record pass, unresolved, or fail with an evidence link and reviewer for each gate. An unresolved gate means defer; a failed gate means decline or request redesign. A high score cannot override a gate.

1. **Willing and accountable:** a consenting sponsor identifies a responsible officer and provides written interest. Interest alone is not authority to run a trial.
2. **Credible authority path:** relevant public bodies and required local reviewers are identified; there is a documented route to authorization without claiming it has been granted.
3. **Rights and continuity:** the scope can be explored without replacing existing benefits, requiring political loyalty, or making essential access depend on employment or digital-only enrollment. No promise precedes a funded delivery plan.
4. **Independent participation:** residents have an accessible way to challenge the scope; proposer conflicts are disclosed and independent review is feasible.
5. **Safe information:** the selection can use organizational records and safe aggregates; no public household-level data is required.

Passing these gates permits comparison for planning only. T02–T07 still control launch readiness.

## Compare candidates consistently

Use 0 = absent or unsupported, 1 = partial evidence with a practical plan to close gaps, and 2 = documented evidence adequate for planning. Score each criterion separately; total range is 0–12. These equal weights are proposed decision rules, not validated predictors of success. Publish any rule change before reviewing candidates and rescore all candidates under it.

| Criterion | What reviewers assess |
| --- | --- |
| Need and fairness | Evidence of unmet need and a plausible way to reach residents currently underserved. |
| Sponsor capability | Staff time, accountable duties, and capacity to coordinate existing providers. |
| Resident influence | Accessible consultation, meaningful dissent, and participation beyond the proposing organization. |
| Delivery feasibility | Realistic bounded scope, supply constraints, and options for offline access and continuity. |
| Financial credibility | Traceable cost assumptions, credible funding investigation, and explicit gaps. Wealth alone does not establish merit. |
| Learning value | Usable baseline, feasible independent evaluation, and lessons relevant to other locations. |

Each reviewer scores independently, cites evidence, and records uncertainty. Publish disagreements alongside the reconciled assessment. Compare only candidates that pass every gate; use scores to inform a reasoned recommendation, not automatic selection. Explain ties and departures from score order. Do not let easier measurement or greater local wealth silently outweigh unmet need.

## Consultation and decision record

Publish an accessible candidate summary and the proposed consultation period before a decision. Set its length and outreach methods with affected residents; explain the choice. Offer an offline route through a consenting local partner. Report who was reached, missing voices, objections, and changes made without exposing identities. Silence is not consent, and a convenience poll does not establish representative support.

Record the outcome as **selected for planning**, **deferred pending evidence**, or **declined with reasons**. Publish the decision owner, reviewers, rubric version, gate results, criterion scores, dissent, conflicts, evidence dates, remaining gaps, and next review date. Permit factual corrections through the correction form and procedural challenges through the governance route. A reviewer who was not responsible for the disputed decision should assess challenges; if none is available, record the unresolved challenge and pause advancement.

T01 closes only when a willing location and accountable sponsor are documented, selection criteria and reasons are public, community input is recorded, and the sponsor acknowledges the planning scope in writing. Selection creates no external spending or service commitment. T02–T07 require their own evidence and authorization before T08 can start.
