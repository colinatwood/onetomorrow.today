# Today governance and data protection design

Prepared 1 November 2026. This is a draft for T05, not an operating institution or legal determination. T02 must establish local coverage and authority first. No sponsor, resident panel, auditor, or data steward is currently assigned.

## Decision rights and accountability

For each decision, publish its scope, legal authority to be confirmed by qualified local reviewers, accountable decision-maker, resident and worker participation, evidence considered, and appeal route. Assign distinct owners for service delivery, finance, data protection, complaints, and independent audit. Where a small organization cannot separate these roles, document the conflict and defer decisions that require independence until an independent reviewer is available.

Resident representatives should participate in setting priorities, reviewing performance, and challenging decisions. Record selection method, term, accessibility and compensation, conflicts, recusal, removal, and replacement rules. Funders, operators, and officials must not control the audit of their own work. A public decision record includes date, options, reasons, dissent, conflicts, review date, and a correction history. Protected essential access is not subject to discretionary budget voting.

## Public accountability

Publish understandable budgets, contract awards and amendments, service standards, aggregate results, complaints and response times, risk decisions, and corrections. Each entry needs an owner, reporting period, source, update date, and reason for any justified redaction. Test the accessibility of public records and offer an offline way to request them. Do not publish names or identifying details of recipients.

## Data lifecycle and access

Before collecting information, document the specific purpose, responsible organization, categories and minimum fields, source, lawful basis to be reviewed locally, recipients, access roles, retention period, deletion method, and contact for corrections. Avoid collecting sensitive data unless needed for an authorized service. Separate identity from analytics, limit access by role, log access, review permissions, encrypt data in transit and at rest, and test restoration. Vendor agreements should state permitted uses, subcontractors, security duties, incident reporting, deletion, and verification.

Publish only aggregates after a disclosure-risk assessment covering small groups, overlapping tables, and repeated releases. Suppression is not a guarantee of anonymity. Mark suppressed or missing results; never replace them with zero. Document how residents can request access or correction, and how a request is handled offline. Retention and deletion periods must be set after local review; do not promise a period that no operator has adopted.

## Complaints, incidents, and continuity

Provide accessible, private complaint intake by more than one channel, a tracking reference, response targets, urgent escalation, and independent appeal. Identify who can authorize interim help and how it is funded. Publish safe aggregate outcomes and unresolved systemic issues.

Maintain an incident register with detection, containment, service continuity, evidence preservation, assessment, notifications as locally required, recovery, and independent review. Assign an incident lead and backup, test offline enrollment and service continuity, and record exercise results. Assess provider withdrawal, compromised credentials, lost records, mistaken exclusion, connectivity failure, and restoration from backup. Do not publicly disclose individual case details.

## Review and completion

For each control record owner, evidence, implementation status, test date, unresolved risks, corrective action, and review date. A written proposal alone is not an operating safeguard. T05 closes only after T02 and qualified local review, assignment of accountable roles, documented resident participation, tested offline and incident procedures, independent audit arrangements, and approved privacy and retention controls. Unresolved serious rights or safety issues block readiness. Feed outcomes and correction measures to T06 and failure exercises to T07; separate authorization is required before T08.
