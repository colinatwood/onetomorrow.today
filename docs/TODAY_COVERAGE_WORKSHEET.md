# Today essential-service coverage worksheet

Prepared 21 October 2026. Status: draft planning aid for T02. No pilot location, sponsor, legal entitlement, or service is established. Complete this worksheet only with the selected community, service operators, and qualified local reviewers. It records proposed choices and questions; it does not state what any jurisdiction's law requires.

T02 depends on [T01 selection](TODAY_PILOT_SELECTION.md). Preparing a blank worksheet does not satisfy that dependency. The [economic transition backlog](TODAY_ECONOMIC_TRANSITION.md) controls subsequent finance, governance, evaluation, and launch gates.

## 1. Name the scope and accountable people

Record the candidate reference, service area, version, preparation date, proposed trial dates, sponsor, decision owner, resident representatives, operator, local reviewer, and next review date. Use **unassigned** or **unknown** rather than an assumed partner. Obtain consent before publishing names; keep personal contact details private.

Separate three things throughout: existing services, a proposed pilot addition, and the long-term universal-access goal. Explain how people outside the proposed scope can obtain existing or emergency help. Publish limitations as prominently as the offer.

## 2. Define each service in terms residents can use

Prepare one record for each service considered. The full basket below is a planning goal; a bounded trial may cover fewer services if it clearly describes the gaps. A payment amount alone does not establish that a service is available.

| Essential need | Questions the local plan must answer |
| --- | --- |
| Nutrition | What food or purchasing support, how often, where redeemable, and with what dietary and disability accommodations? |
| Water and sanitation | What safe supply and sanitation service, at which locations, with what interruption response? |
| Secure shelter | What accommodation or support, duration, suitability, accessibility, and response to displacement or provider withdrawal? |
| Basic energy | What usable service, seasonal and medical adjustments, and response when a household cannot receive the proposed supply? |
| Essential healthcare | What services and referrals, available providers, waiting times, urgent-care route, and exclusions? Qualified local clinical reviewers must define the scope. |
| Connectivity | What access, equipment or assisted alternative, reliability, and offline route for every essential process? |

For every record include: units and period; quality standard and its source; population; adjustments for household size, age, disability, medical needs, and climate; delivery method; service location and hours; supplier capacity; household charges if any; full cost assumptions; waiting time; maximum interruption; backup; complaint route; responsible owner; evidence date; and unknowns. Label numerical values as proposed until locally reviewed and authorized. Connect costs and capacity to T03 and T04 using the same units and population.

## 3. Resolve access questions without public personal records

Use a matrix with columns **proposed rule**, **reason**, **local review needed**, **accessible alternative**, **owner**, and **status**. Include residents already using services, newcomers, refugees, undocumented people, people without conventional identity documents, people without fixed addresses, people without phones or bank accounts, and people needing an authorized helper. Discuss categories with local reviewers; do not collect people's status to fill out this planning worksheet.

For each category specify enrollment steps, proportionate evidence, assisted and offline access, emergency handling, waiting lists, correction of mistaken records, and an appeal route. Identify who has authority to resolve eligibility questions. Keep unresolved restrictions visible; do not describe coverage as universal while people are excluded.

Proposed safeguards for local adoption:

- Essential access does not require employment, political support, trading an entitlement, or accepting an automated job assignment.
- An algorithm may flag a question but cannot make an unreviewable termination decision.
- A lost phone, disputed identity, or network outage has a documented human and offline resolution path.
- Any anti-fraud check has a stated purpose, proportionate evidence, a correction route, and a review of exclusion risk.

## 4. Check existing benefits before offering support

For each affected program record its responsible agency, the proposed pilot interaction, the evidence source and date, the question sent for qualified review, the answer and its limits, and the owner of unresolved work. Cover eligibility, income or asset treatment, household charges, reporting, taxation where relevant, and service substitution. Mark **unknown** when no reliable answer is available.

Compare the household's expected position before and after the addition, including lost support, fees, travel, paperwork, and timing. Use hypothetical or safely aggregated examples for public reporting. Never promise that existing benefits are protected based on an unverified assumption. Defer the affected offer until material interactions have been reviewed and a funded response to foreseeable harm is agreed. Finance any proposed compensation explicitly in T04.

## 5. Make complaints and appeals usable

Before launch the local plan must assign intake staff, a service decision owner, and a reviewer independent of the disputed decision. Publish plain-language reasons for decisions, a non-secret case reference, accessible contact methods, proposed response deadlines, escalation, urgent handling, and a way to track a complaint without internet access. Explain how a resident can use a helper without publishing sensitive information.

Specify interim support during a dispute, its limits, who can authorize it, and its funding. Describe what happens if a deadline is missed or no independent reviewer is available. Participation in evaluation or a complaint must not become a condition for essential access. Publish aggregate complaint outcomes only after a privacy review.

## 6. Plan shortage and closure before enrollment

For each service document shortage priorities, who may activate them, notice methods, backup suppliers, emergency purchasing authority, reserve needs, and the maximum sustainable interruption. Protect people with urgent needs and disclose the limits of the proposed response. Test assumptions in T07 rather than treating a written backup as proven capacity.

For planned closure or sponsor withdrawal specify notice, transition help, receiving providers where agreed, remaining funding, outstanding complaints, and secure record handling. Do not name a receiving provider as committed without agreement. T04 must cost the continuity plan; T05 must define retention and deletion; T06 must define stop triggers. Stopping expansion and abruptly removing support are different decisions.

## 7. Review and handoff

Use **draft**, **awaiting evidence**, **reviewed with conditions**, or **ready for sponsor decision** for each section. Record reviewer, date, evidence, conditions, dissent, corrections, and next review date. A blank field, unresolved serious access issue, unknown material benefit interaction, or unassigned appeal owner prevents readiness.

T02 closes only after T01 is complete and the community panel and qualified local reviewers have documented the basket, coverage rules, emergency access, appeals, existing-benefit interactions, and responsible public authority. Publish an accessible summary and justified redactions. Feed capacity gaps into T03, costs into T04, data duties into T05, outcome measures into T06, and failure cases into T07. Closing T02 does not authorize a trial; all P0 gates remain necessary for T08.
