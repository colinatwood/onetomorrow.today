One mission · Three horizons

Shape today. Consider tomorrow. Build the future.

OneTomorrow organizes the work ahead into three distinct but connected branches: redesign the global economy as AGI changes production, establish principled relations among biological and artificial entities, and build the Ark systems that can carry life responsibly into space.

Today

Global economic shift in the age of AGI

Build the institutions, ownership models, economic floor, and democratic safeguards needed to turn technological abundance into widely shared freedom.

  • Work, income, and transition security
  • Broad ownership of productive systems
  • Competition, access, and public accountability

Entity consideration and the future of cybernetics

Explore artificial agency and human–AI symbiosis while preserving the autonomy, consent, identity, and uniqueness of biological and technological entities.

  • Voluntary, reciprocal symbiosis

Future

Ark creation and the exploration of space

Develop the mission architecture, spacecraft, habitat, protection, and closed-loop life support needed for long-duration exploration beyond Earth.

  • Reference mission and whole-ship budgets
  • Habitat ecology and crew survival
  • Evidence-driven demonstrators

Shared foundation

Different work, one standard of legitimacy.

Every branch operates through public evidence, transparent assumptions, voluntary participation, reversible experiments, clear boundaries, and institutions that can be audited. No horizon becomes an excuse to neglect people living now, erase the individuality of future entities, or substitute speculation for engineering proof.
