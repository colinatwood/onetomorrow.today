One mission · Three horizons

Shape today. Consider tomorrow. Build the future.

OneTomorrow organizes the work ahead into three connected branches: prepare the economy for artificial general intelligence (AGI), decide how people and increasingly independent AI systems should work together, and build the Ark spacecraft systems needed to carry life responsibly into space.

Today

Global economic shift in the age of AGI

Build fair rules for work, income, ownership, and public oversight as advanced AI changes how goods and services are produced.

  • Security during changes to work and income
  • Broad ownership of wealth-producing systems
  • Fair competition and public oversight

Tomorrow

Entity consideration and the future of cybernetics

Explore how people and increasingly independent AI might cooperate. This includes cybernetics—technology that connects human bodies and machines—without giving up consent or individual identity.

  • Identity, choice, and responsibility for AI
  • Safe links between people and technology
  • Voluntary cooperation that benefits both sides

Future

Ark creation and the exploration of space

Develop the mission plan, spacecraft, living space, protection, and recycling systems needed to keep a crew alive far from Earth.

  • A clear mission and complete ship budgets
  • A safe habitat and healthy crew
  • Small tests that build toward flight

Shared foundation

Different work, one standard of proof.

Every branch publishes its evidence and assumptions, keeps participation voluntary, tests changes on a small scale, and sets clear limits. None may neglect people living now, erase the identity of future beings, or treat an untested idea as proven engineering.
